CURRENTLY IMPLEMENTED:
- COMBO SYSTEM
  - 3-Hit Combo To Finisher (Stun)
  - custom finishers for certain weapon types
- PARRY SYSTEM
  - correct timing with the shield allows for a parry that knocks back and stuns the enemy
- SNEAK TIME
  - sneak decreases your enemy detection much more strongly
  - backstab does extra damage
  - silent finisher with massive extra damage if close enough while not spotted
- DODGE
  - direction dodge in direction of movement
- Stamina System
  - dodge, parry, run, shield, finishers, sneak time, all take up stamina



POTENTIAL NEXT VERSION UPDATES:
- DODGE
  - PERFECT DODGE WINDOW: reuse the parry's "tight window" pattern: dodging right as a hit would land (not just anytime) refunds stamina or opens a brief counter-attack opportunity, vs. a normal dodge that just avoids damage at full cost.
- STAMINA/DODGE
  - ARMOR WEIGHT: heavier armor = shorter dodge distance and higher stamina costs, light armor = cheaper/longer dodges. Real gear-choice tension, and it's just reading an armor value you already have access to.
- BREAK METER
  - BREAK METER: (on tough enemies) separate from your existing stagger-on-finisher: repeated hits build a meter on a specific enemy, and filling it forces a guaranteed critical opening (Sekiro-style), distinct from the takedown since it works in the open, not just from stealth.
- HEAL ITEM
  - LIMITED-USE HEAL: (an Estus-alike) a craftable/rechargeable item with a small number of charges, refilled at a bed or specific block.

- COMBO COUNTER
  - COMBO COUNTER: progressively keep an ongoing combo that when reaching higher numbers, increases speed of attacks, movement, regains stamina, etc.
  - STREAK TAKEDOWN: once your combo counter hits some threshold, unlock a flashier AoE or multi-enemy execute for a few seconds, mirroring Arkham's Fear Takedown unlock.

- EXTRA/EXPERIMENTAL
  - HIT-STOP: a very brief freeze-frame (slow nearby mobs for a couple ticks, or hitch the player's own animation) on a finisher/takedown hit. Cheap to build, disproportionately satisfying, the single most "Arkham" feeling thing on this list.
